Privacy

oytc uses YouTube API Services. Using it means agreeing to the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google's handling of your data is covered by the Google Privacy Policy.

oytc stores your Google account email and, for each connected channel, its ID, title, handle, and a Google refresh token. Refresh tokens are encrypted at rest. YouTube data and analytics are fetched live when requested and are never stored.

Disconnecting a channel deletes its token and revokes it with Google. You can also remove oytc's access at any time in your Google account permissions.

Questions go to the owner via davis7.sh.
